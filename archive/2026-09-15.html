AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

case study · Concurrency
Establishing an AI-Ready Data Governance Foundation

Before you can safely point an AI assistant at a company's files, you need to know two things: where the sensitive data actually lives, and who can already see it. This write-up walks through a governance engagement that mapped both first with Microsoft Purview — scanning for sensitive data across Microsoft 365, tightening who could access what, and watching for insider risk — before any AI tool was switched on, rather than patching access control after an assistant was already reading things it shouldn't.

For the roadmap Sequencing is the lesson: a visibility-and-access pass before the AI rollout, not after, is what avoids the retrofit costs that show up once an assistant is found reading something it was never supposed to reach.
