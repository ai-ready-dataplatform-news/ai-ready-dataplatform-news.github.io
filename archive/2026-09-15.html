AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    AI agents used to breach 395 organisations via PaperCut

    GreyNoise traced a single attacker running hundreds of AI agents on OpenAI's Codex and a DeepSeek model to exploit two PaperCut CVEs, compromising 440 instances across 395 organisations in 48 countries. The Register · 2026-09-10

    Agent-facing endpoints now need the identity checks and rate limits of any internet-facing API — this attacker automated exploitation itself, not just reconnaissance.

  2. 02
    OpenAI's ChatGPT Work now queries the warehouse directly

    The new Data agent in ChatGPT Work connects to Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Redshift, ClickHouse, MongoDB and Datadog, answers plain-English questions, and publishes editable dashboards into Tableau, Power BI and Sigma. OpenAI · 2026-09-10

    A general-purpose chat product is now a credible substitute for the governed semantic layer a platform team builds in-house — the buy-vs-build case for that layer just got harder to make.

  3. 03
    UK closes its call for evidence on data rules for AI

    DSIT's call for evidence on whether UK data regulation supports or blocks AI and other data-intensive technologies closed on 9 September, gathering practical examples rather than position papers. GOV.UK / DSIT · 2026-09-09

    The responses decide between further guidance, targeted changes, or fundamental reform of UK data regulation — worth watching before locking in a compliance roadmap that assumes the status quo.
