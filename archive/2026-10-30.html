AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    SAP Business Data Cloud goes Iceberg-native with Dremio inside

    At TechEd Berlin SAP added Dremio-based native Apache Iceberg and Polaris support and federated query to Business Data Cloud, bundled Databricks tooling as SAP Databricks, and introduced an AI-assisted Ontology Manager in beta that lets Joule Studio agents reach BDC data products directly. SAP News · 2026-10-29

    A platform team that treats SAP as just another source to ingest now faces a vendor that wants to own the lakehouse, the catalog and the business ontology over that data; the build-vs-federate call on ERP data needs revisiting before the next renewal.

  2. 02
    Prophecy DataGPT turns chat-built workflows into scheduled data products

    Prophecy launched DataGPT, which lets business analysts build data workflows from inside ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot; once approved, a workflow ships as a data product that runs as governed, repeatable code on Snowflake, Databricks or BigQuery on a schedule. AIwire · 2026-10-28

    When an analyst's chat session can mint a scheduled data product, the platform's approval gate becomes the whole governance model; a data product owner needs a clear rule for who may promote a chat-built workflow into the governed catalogue, and what it must carry when it does.

  3. 03
    Cloudera Blueprints package governed agent workflows for regulated data

    At EVOLVE26 Cloudera launched Blueprints, prebuilt AI packages combining NVIDIA Agent Toolkit reference workflows with Cloudera's governance, lineage and security, deployable on-premises, in public cloud or at the edge, with financial services, healthcare and telecoms as the first targets. TechTarget · 2026-10-28

    For regulated estates that cannot move sensitive data to a model, this is a packaged argument for bringing agents to the data instead; analysts note the hard part is adapting a blueprint to the firm's own definitions, which is where a governed semantic layer earns its place on the roadmap.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    A third of enterprise data is now written by AI

    AvePoint's survey of 750 IT leaders in financial services, healthcare and government estimates 35.5% of enterprise data is now AI-generated, heading for 42.1% within a year, and found generative AI and agent rollouts delayed by close to six months on average, with data security and governance gaps cited ahead of budget. AvePoint, with Osterman Research · 2026-06-29

    If a third of what lands on the platform was written by a model, lineage and quality checks have to say so: tagging AI-generated content at ingestion becomes a governance requirement before it feeds the next model, not a nice-to-have.

podcast · Experiencing Data with Brian T. O'Neill, episode 194 · 2026-05-12
AI for BI: Juan Sequeda on preparing your analytics to work with LLMs

A language model answering a business question is guessing what words like 'customer' or 'active policy' mean, because the tables only hold columns and numbers. A semantic layer writes those meanings down once: what each business term is, how each measure is calculated, and how the tables join. Sequeda's point is to start from the job the person is trying to do, model the data around it, and give the model those written-down meanings, rather than starting from the chatbot.

For the roadmap Conversational access sits on top of definitions, not instead of them, so the agreed business definitions in a gold layer are the real product and the chat interface is one way of consuming it; that argues for funding semantic modelling and ownership of definitions ahead of more interfaces.
