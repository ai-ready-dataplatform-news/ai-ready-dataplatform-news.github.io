AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Databricks AI Search now scales to thousands of QPS

    Databricks made high-QPS scaling for AI Search generally available on July 28, letting a single target-QPS setting take a vector index from prototype to full production throughput with built-in observability. Databricks Blog · 2026-07-28

    Closes a real buy-vs-build gap for production-grade vector search inside the lakehouse — worth weighing against a dedicated search vendor for retrieval-heavy agent workloads.

  2. 02
    EU publishes AI Act transparency rules before Aug 2

    The European Commission adopted guidelines on July 20 for the AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations, which take effect August 2 with fines of up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover. European Commission · 2026-07-20

    Any platform feature that lets an AI system interact with users or generate synthetic content needs a live disclosure mechanism in days, not on next quarter's roadmap.

  3. 03
    MCP's biggest spec revision makes the protocol stateless

    The 2026-07-28 Model Context Protocol specification rewrites the transport core to be stateless, adds a formal extensions framework, and gives three deprecated features a 12-month runway. Model Context Protocol Blog · 2026-07-28

    Any MCP servers already wired into agent-to-data access now have a migration clock running — budget the rework before the deprecation window closes.

  4. 04
    Gartner: AI platform spending to grow 63% in 2026

    Gartner forecasts worldwide spending on AI platforms and models will reach $64 billion in 2026, up 63.4% from $39 billion in 2025, with GenAI models growing fastest at 117%. Gartner · 2026-07-20

    That's the scale of budget vendors and competitors are pricing in this year — a useful external benchmark when building the investment case for the platform roadmap.

  5. 05
    Iceberg contributors push to drop equality deletes in V4

    Iceberg contributors opened a formal proposal this week to remove equality deletes from the V4 spec, while Polaris debated how much consistency its persistence layer owes users. Apache Data Lakehouse Weekly · 2026-07-23

    A V4 spec change to delete semantics is a compatibility question for any table-maintenance job written against current Iceberg behavior — worth flagging before it lands.
