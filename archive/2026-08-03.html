AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Snowflake launches Cortex AI Gateway for agent governance

    Snowflake's new Cortex AI Gateway centrally manages agent access, authentication and cost monitoring across more than 100 MCP servers. Snowflake · 2026-07-28

    Snowflake shops now have a native control-plane option for agent governance, worth weighing against building MCP access controls in-house.

  2. 02
    EU AI Act transparency and GPAI enforcement now live

    From August 2, Article 50 AI-content labeling duties and full enforcement powers over general-purpose AI models took effect, with fines up to €15M or 3% of global turnover. European Commission · 2026-08-02

    Any customer-facing generative feature reaching EU users needs an audited disclosure trail now, not as a future roadmap line.

  3. 03
    Airbyte adds workspaces and write access for data agents

    Airbyte's Agentic Data Platform now supports isolated team workspaces and lets agents create and update HubSpot records under least-privilege, role-based permissions. BigDATAwire · 2026-07-29

    Write-capable agents raise the stakes on connector governance — confirm write scopes are audit-logged before greenlighting agent access to production systems.

  4. 04
    Cloudera: healthcare data gaps stall AI despite visibility

    87% of healthcare respondents know where their data resides, yet Cloudera's Data Readiness Index finds governance and integration gaps still block operationalizing AI at scale. Cloudera / GlobeNewswire · 2026-07-28

    Knowing where data lives isn't readiness — the gap between visibility and governance is the roadmap line item to size before promising AI timelines.

  5. 05
    Apache Polaris pulls 1.7.0 release over licensing gaps

    Polaris withdrew its 1.7.0 release candidate after 44 of 269 staged jars were found missing required LICENSE and NOTICE files. Apache Data Lakehouse Weekly · 2026-07-29

    A catalog this central to Iceberg interoperability shipping with incomplete license metadata is a vendor-risk flag worth a quick compliance check before the next upgrade.
