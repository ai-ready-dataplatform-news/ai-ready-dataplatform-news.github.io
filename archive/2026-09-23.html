AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Progress Software completes $400M purchase of Domo

    Progress Software finalized its $400 million purchase of Domo's AI and data platform business on September 22, 2026. TechTarget

    Another mid-market BI and data platform vendor exits as a standalone company — a fresh data point for vendor-risk and consolidation clauses in any multi-year platform contract.

  2. 02
    Egnyte launches a context layer for AI agents

    Egnyte introduced its Context Layer on September 15, 2026, mapping relationships across content, people, projects and systems so agents get business context without redundant processing. GlobeNewswire (Egnyte)

    Every major vendor is now shipping some version of a context layer for agents — the differentiator for a buyer isn't the concept but whether it plugs into a catalog and lineage a platform already governs, or becomes one more disconnected metadata store to reconcile.

  3. 03
    Gartner: most audit teams use AI without a strategy

    Gartner found 93% of audit functions now use AI, but 60% still lack a formal strategy governing that use, as of September 10, 2026. Gartner

    The same ungoverned-adoption pattern shows up in audit as in data platforms: teams reach for AI before the operating model or accountability is decided — a caution against judging any function, including a platform still in its foundation phase, on usage alone.

  4. 04
    Patra launches AI-run managed services for insurers

    Patra introduced agentic AI-run managed services on September 17, 2026, where a single client trigger runs an entire insurance servicing workflow with built-in quality checks before human review. FinTech Global

    An outsourced agentic workflow like this depends entirely on the data behind it being governed and current — a preview of the bar a data platform will be judged against once operations, not just analytics, run on top of it.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Databricks adds GPT-6 Astra to Unity Gateway

    Databricks made OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra available through Unity Gateway as a day-zero release on September 3, 2026, routing every call through the same governance, audit trail and cost controls as its other models. Analytics India Magazine

    Routing a frontier model through the same governance layer as everything else in Unity Catalog keeps model access inside the existing access-control and audit story — the alternative is another unmanaged integration point outside the platform's own governance.

  2. 02
    OpenAI ships a model built to detect PII

    OpenAI launched Privacy Filter on September 18, 2026, a small model built for context-aware detection of personal data in unstructured text at high throughput. OpenAI

    A dedicated PII-detection model separate from the generation model is a candidate building block for the boundary between a governed gold layer and agentic consumption — worth evaluating against whatever masking and classification tooling the platform already runs.

podcast · Experiencing Data w/ Brian T. O'Neill
Why Powerful AI & Analytics Products Feel Useless to Buyers

Two different people decide whether a B2B analytics or AI product survives: the buyer, who signs the contract and needs to defend the purchase with low perceived risk, and the user, who just wants their daily work easier or safer. The episode's point is that model accuracy and clean analytics data have become table stakes — nearly every serious vendor now has them — so they no longer win a deal. What actually wins or loses one is whether the buyer can see, in plain terms, how the tool changes a decision they make, and whether the user knows what to do with an answer the moment they see it. A technically excellent product that never translates its outputs into that story reads as a black box, and buyers walk from black boxes.

For the roadmap The same translation gap applies inside a platform team's own walls: a governed gold layer or a Genie connection is 'powerful' by engineering measures, but the people deciding whether to keep funding it — product managers, analysts, sponsors — judge it by whether it visibly changed a decision, not by lineage completeness or query latency. It's a reason to report a foundation-phase platform on decisions unblocked rather than on adoption counts alone.
