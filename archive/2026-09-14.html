AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    EU AI Office Opens First Wave of Compliance Audits

    The European AI Office and regulators CNIL, BfDI and AESIA began inspecting high-risk AI systems this month, starting with resume screening, credit assessment and healthcare triage tools. Cubbbix

    Any platform feeding models into those three sectors needs audit-ready Article 11 technical documentation now, not at the next compliance sprint.

  2. 02
    Iceberg 1.12.0 Nears Its Release Candidate

    Apache Iceberg's 1.12.0 milestone is down to its last blocking PRs, with committer Neelesh Salian planning an RC0 cut and a longer vote window around the US holiday. DEV Community

    Hilbert curve clustering landing in 1.12.0 is a concrete reason to test read and write performance before locking in this quarter's catalog and engine versions.

  3. 03
    Salesforce Launches Seven Named Agentforce Agents

    Salesforce introduced seven named Agentforce agents ahead of Dreamforce, most generally available, alongside a Trusted Enterprise AI Harness meant to govern agents across platforms. Unite.AI

    A governance layer aimed at multi-vendor agent sprawl is now a real feature to evaluate, not a roadmap slide, when scoping this year's agent platform bet.

  4. 04
    AAIF Launches Its First MCP Certification

    The Agentic AI Foundation launched the Model Context Protocol Associate certification as the community gathered in Amsterdam for AGNTCon and MCPCon Europe. PR Newswire

    A vendor-neutral MCP credential gives platform teams a hiring signal that didn't exist while every MCP integration was still bespoke.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Prophecy Adds Reviewable AI Agents to Data Prep

    Prophecy shipped AI agents for visual data prep with a step-by-step review flow, built on Claude Code, and extended support from Databricks to Snowflake. AiThority

    A review-before-approve pattern for agent-made pipeline changes is a template worth borrowing before letting agents touch production transforms unsupervised.

  2. 02
    Omni Ships AI Alerts and Just-In-Time MCP Auth

    Omni's September demo round added AI-generated alerts, emailed dashboard summaries and just-in-time MCP authentication alongside a personalized homepage. Omni Docs

    Just-in-time auth for MCP connections is the kind of access-control detail that decides whether a semantic layer is safe to expose to agents at all.

  3. 03
    Survey: Latin America's AI Governance Gap

    RSM's survey of 461 business leaders found 40% already implementing AI and 68% of adopters planning to scale within a year, but only 18% with formal governance frameworks in place. RSM Latin America

    The ambition-versus-governance gap this survey quantifies is the same gap that turns a scaling plan into a compliance incident.

Data Governance Edge: Stand Out in the AI Era

Data governance is usually described as a set of rules — who can see what, who owns which table. This course frames it instead as an operating model: a specific group of people (owners, stewards, a council) with specific decision rights, who keep a live map of where data comes from and what shape it's supposed to be in, so that when something breaks, there's a name attached to fixing it rather than a shared shrug. Metadata and lineage are the map; the operating model is who's on the hook for keeping the map true.

For the roadmap A platform can be technically AI-ready and still fail in production if no one owns the decision rights when a model consumes data nobody was accountable for — the operating-model gap this course targets is often the real blocker, not the tooling.
