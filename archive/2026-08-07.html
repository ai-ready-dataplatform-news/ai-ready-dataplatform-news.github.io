AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Databricks Unity AI Gateway reaches general availability

    Unity AI Gateway is now GA, giving Databricks customers one governance layer for AI cost, security and model routing across agents, tools and MCP servers, with over a quadrillion tokens already routed through it in the past year. StartupHub.ai · 2026-08-04

    A governance layer bundled into the platform narrows the buy-vs-build case for a standalone AI gateway — worth weighing against anything already scoped in-house for agent cost and access control.

  2. 02
    California's AI content-watermarking law becomes operative

    SB 942 took effect August 2, requiring any generative AI system with over one million California users to embed a hidden provenance watermark in AI-generated images, video and audio and offer a free public detection tool, backed by $5,000-per-day fines. Tech Times · 2026-08-02

    Any product surface that generates media for California users now needs a watermarking and provenance pipeline on the roadmap, not just a policy update — worth an audit of which features are actually in scope.

  3. 03
    Amazon Bedrock cuts OpenAI GPT-5.6 pricing up to 80%

    Amazon Bedrock cut on-demand inference prices for OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna by 80% and Terra by 20%, with existing customers automatically migrated to the new rates by August 1 — Luna now runs $0.20 per million input tokens. AWS · 2026-07-30

    An 80% cut on a frontier-class model reshapes the build-vs-buy math for high-volume classification and content workloads that were priced out of GPT-class models a quarter ago.

  4. 04
    ShinyHunters adds three Salesforce-linked breaches to leak site

    ShinyHunters listed Questel, Alcon and Lumenis on its extortion site, claiming more than 47 million combined Salesforce records containing PII and over 300GB of internal corporate data taken through the same integration pattern across all three. BreachNews · 2026-08-01

    A repeat breach pattern through one SaaS integration is a reason to audit connected-app scopes against Salesforce-adjacent data flows this quarter rather than wait for a vendor advisory.

  5. 05
    BigHand acquires legal data-intelligence platform Ayora

    BigHand acquired Ayora, an eight-person AI pricing and cost-intelligence platform for law firms, folding its data and pricing expertise directly into BigHand's matter-pricing and business-intelligence products. Law.com · 2026-08-04

    A vertical AI-data point solution absorbed this cheaply and this fast is the pattern worth watching among your own niche vendors before a renewal turns into an acquisition surprise.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Databricks completes Panther deal for a security lakehouse

    Databricks closed its acquisition of Panther, folding SOC detection-as-code and agentic triage workflows into the lakehouse so security telemetry can be retained at scale in open formats instead of a separate, costlier SIEM. Databricks · 2026-08-03

    Security data joining the same governed lakehouse as everything else previews what 'AI-ready' will mean for every other siloed data domain still sitting outside the platform.

  2. 02
    Snowflake's Multi-party Approval reaches general availability

    Multi-party Approval is now GA, requiring two or more authorizations before any destructive account operation can proceed, so a single set of hijacked administrative credentials can no longer wipe data or alter configuration alone. Snowflake · 2026-08-04

    A four-eyes control built into the platform is a concrete bar to hold any governed-data promise against — worth checking whether 'clean and governed' in your own roadmap actually survives a single compromised admin.

case study · AtScale — Semantic Layer Summit 2026 (on-demand) · 2026-05-20
Blue Yonder's semantic-layer rebuild, told as a session

A semantic layer is a single, shared definition of what a business term like "revenue" or "active customer" actually means — the calculation, the filters, the exceptions — stored once and reused everywhere, so a dashboard, a spreadsheet and an AI agent all get the same number instead of three different ones built from three different queries. Without it, every tool re-derives its own version of the truth, and an AI agent asked a business question either guesses or gets it wrong with total confidence.

For the roadmap Blue Yonder rebuilt 800+ tables into one governed semantic layer before wiring any AI agent to it — the sequencing is the lesson worth stealing: get the shared metric layer right first, or every agent shipped afterward inherits the same reconciliation mess analysts already live with.
