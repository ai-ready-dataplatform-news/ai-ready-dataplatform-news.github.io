    RavenDB Launches Quill for AI Agents on SQL

    RavenDB launched Quill on September 8, a context layer that connects directly to an organization's existing SQL database so production AI agents can query it without migrating the system of record. TechStartups · 2026-09-08

    It's a concrete data point in the agent-context-layer land grab — vendors building ways for agents to work against governed SQL data in place — worth weighing against building the same bridge yourself with an in-house semantic or MCP layer.

case study · Stanford Graduate School of Business
Snowflake in 2026 (A): All In On Enterprise AI

A cloud data warehouse is basically a huge, fast filing cabinet: you put your company's data in and query it back out. An 'AI Data Cloud' is the same filing cabinet, except AI models and agents are now allowed to reach in and work with the files directly, under the same locks and access rules a person would have to follow, instead of the files getting copied out to some separate AI tool first. This case follows Snowflake's leadership trying to make that shift stick, redesigning the business model, the partner ecosystem, and how the engineering org itself worked along the way.

For the roadmap Worth reading before writing your own AI-readiness roadmap: it's a real account of what it costs organizationally, not just technically, to turn a data platform into something agents can act on directly, and where a consumption-based business model and ecosystem bets either hold up or crack under that shift.
