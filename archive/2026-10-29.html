AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Bristol Myers Squibb licenses Tempus oncology data through 2031

    On 23 October Bristol Myers Squibb signed a multi-year licence to Tempus's multimodal oncology data through 2031, with access to its agentic Lens platform, while Pathos AI agreed a five-year, $55 million licence to a neurology dataset Tempus will build from hundreds of thousands of patients; the BMS price was not disclosed. Tempus · 2026-10-23

    Curated, consented data sold as a multi-year licence plus a platform to work it shows the packaging a data product vendor can charge for, which is the comparison a platform PM needs when arguing whether a governed dataset should be priced as an asset rather than costed as storage.

  2. 02
    Monte Carlo launches Bayes to fix agents and their data

    Monte Carlo released Bayes on 28 October, an agent that detects and remediates problems across production AI agents and the data they depend on, citing a South American financial institution that found 200% more incidents than its internal monitoring with 80% fewer false positives, and Snowflake using it for customers' Cortex agents. SiliconANGLE · 2026-10-28

    Once quality tooling watches the agent and the table together, a team validating conversational access over a gold layer can treat agent failures as data incidents with an owner, rather than a separate monitoring stack to buy before the pilot moves on.

  3. 03
    Meta buys Sundial and shuts it to outside customers

    Meta acquired AI analytics startup Sundial, reported on 23 October, folding co-founders Julie Zhuo and Chandra Narayanan into its new data analytics tooling and agents unit under chief data officer Alex Schultz, with the natural-language analysis product to stop serving external customers, OpenAI among them, once the deal closes. Axios · 2026-10-23

    A conversational analytics tool that disappears on acquisition is the vendor-risk case for keeping metric definitions and warehouse access in the governed platform layer, so the agent on top can be swapped without rebuilding the business logic.

  4. 04
    Zoho Analytics builds semantic and ontology layers in

    Zoho repositioned Zoho Analytics as an agentic analytics platform on 28 October with native semantic and ontology layers, an MCP server as its headless option alongside a Claude plugin and command-line interface, and Ask Zia rebuilt as a multi-agent system of six pre-built sub-agents on the same semantic models. diginomica · 2026-10-28

    When a mid-market BI tool ships the semantic layer and the agent protocol in one release, the buy-versus-build question for an enterprise platform shifts from whether to have a governed semantic layer to which tool owns it and which agents may read it.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Snowflake's online feature store reaches general availability

    Snowflake made its Online Feature Store generally available on 20 October, serving the latest feature values for real-time uses such as fraud detection and personalisation from a managed Snowflake Postgres layer, with resizable service sizes, Snowsight monitoring of refresh, latency and ingest, and availability limited to AWS and Azure commercial regions. Snowflake release notes · 2026-10-20

    Serving model features from the same governed store as the analytical tables removes one copy of the data to keep in sync, which belongs in the readiness plan as a test: can the gold layer feed a live model without a second pipeline.

  2. 02
    Redshift extends federated permissions across multiple warehouses

    Redshift patch 206, dated 27 October, adds Federated Permissions support in multi-warehouse setups, data sharing for stored procedures and opt-in SUPER values up to 16,000,000 bytes, and fixes a slowdown in cross-warehouse queries. Amazon Redshift cluster versions · 2026-10-27

    Permissions defined once and honoured across every warehouse that reads shared data close one of the gaps that keeps a governed dataset from being reused safely, so it is worth checking against how access is enforced where Redshift consumers sit beside the lakehouse.

course · Coursera, Packt
Managing Data as a Product: Scalable Data Architectures

Most companies treat data as a by-product: one team makes it as a side effect of running something else, and whoever needs it digs it out and cleans it themselves. Treating data as a product flips that. A named team owns a dataset the way a product team owns an app, decides who its users are, promises how fresh and correct it will be, and is judged on whether people actually use it. This course covers how to design, build and manage data products like that across a large organisation where many teams publish them.

For the roadmap It gives the vocabulary for turning a first governed dataset into a repeatable product with an owner, a promise and a measure of use, which is the frame a roadmap needs before the second and third domains are onboarded.
