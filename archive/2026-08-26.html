AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Bedrock AgentCore web search adds date filters, EU/APAC

    AWS added domain and published-date filtering to Bedrock AgentCore's built-in web search tool and expanded it from the US to Europe (Ireland) and Asia Pacific (Tokyo). AWS What's New · 2026-08-20

    Agents can now be scoped to trusted domains and a recency window per call — a concrete lever for keeping retrieval-augmented answers inside a compliance boundary instead of the open web.

  2. 02
    McKinsey: AI's earnings payoff still isn't showing up

    McKinsey surveyed 1,719 leaders for its 2026 State of AI report and found just 37% attribute any EBIT impact to AI use — essentially flat against 2025 despite heavier deployment. The Register, on McKinsey's State of AI 2026 · 2026-08-25

    The budget case for platform investment still can't lean on a rising ROI curve — it has to be told through specific use cases and unit economics, not aggregate adoption numbers.

  3. 03
    Gartner: AI splits IT spending into winners and losers

    Gartner raised its 2026 worldwide IT spending forecast to $6.37 trillion (+14.2% YoY), but says AI substitution is cutting into implementation and testing services, leaving infrastructure, data-and-AI, and security as the only markets clearly positioned for growth. The Register, on Gartner · 2026-08-20

    If services budgets for SAP/Salesforce/Oracle implementation work are about to shrink, that's spend freed up to redirect toward the data-and-AI platform build — worth raising before next year's plan gets drafted around last year's services mix.

  4. 04
    Enterprises are paying down two years of AI governance debt

    McKinsey's 2026 AI Trust Maturity Survey puts average responsible-AI maturity at 2.3 out of 4, with only about 30% of organisations past level three on governance and agentic-AI controls even as deployment accelerates. VentureBeat, on McKinsey's 2026 AI Trust Maturity Survey · 2026-08-23

    The organisations pulling ahead are narrowing what agents are allowed to do rather than widening it — reason to budget for guardrails and scoped permissions alongside the next round of agent rollouts, not after.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Databricks lets consumer-tier users query Unity AI Gateway

    Databricks opened an opt-in public preview letting consumer-access users query Unity AI Gateway's model and model-provider services directly, and is telling admins to set budgets and rate limits now, before the preview becomes the default. Databricks release notes · 2026-08-22

    Widening who can call the model gateway is a cost-control problem before it's a capability one — worth setting spend guardrails on this ahead of general availability rather than after the first surprise bill.

  2. 02
    Snowflake pins agent versions inside Cortex evaluations

    Snowflake shipped version targeting for Cortex Agent and Cortex Analyst evaluations, letting a scored run pin a specific agent version, alias or judge-model version instead of scoring against a mutable live version. Snowflake release notes · 2026-08-21

    Scheduled and CI/CD evaluations that silently re-scored against whatever was live were never really reproducible — pinning the version is what makes 'we test our agents' a claim the platform can actually back up.

  3. 03
    Power BI's August update adds granular semantic model refresh

    Power BI's August 2026 feature summary adds granular semantic model refresh controls and generally-available visual calculations, alongside modern visual defaults and OneLake image URLs in visuals. Microsoft Fabric Community · 2026-08-24

    Refresh control at the semantic-model level, not just the dataset, is the kind of unglamorous plumbing that decides whether an AI agent reading through Power BI ever answers from stale numbers.

video · AtScale — Semantic Layer Summit 2026 (on-demand) · 2026-05-20
Scale at Speed: Carrefour France's 3,000 KPIs move to one semantic layer

A semantic layer is a single place where a business defines what its numbers mean — 'revenue', 'active customer', 'churn' — once, so every dashboard, report and AI agent that asks for 'revenue' gets back the same number, worked out the same way. Without one, every tool and every person writing their own query ends up with a slightly different answer to the same question, and nobody notices until two reports disagree in a meeting.

For the roadmap This session covers migrating 3,000 existing KPI definitions into one governed layer without a big-bang rewrite — a practical playbook for any platform sitting on years of BI-tool-specific metric definitions and wondering how that migration would actually go.
