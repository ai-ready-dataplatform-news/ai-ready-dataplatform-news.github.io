AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Snowflake launches Cortex AI Gateway for agents

    Snowflake's July 28 Cortex AI Gateway centralizes access control, authentication and cost monitoring for both native and third-party agents across more than 100 MCP servers. Snowflake · 2026-07-28

    A native control-plane for agent governance narrows the case for building an in-house MCP gateway — worth a build-vs-buy pass before the next agent rollout.

  2. 02
    EU AI Act enforcement powers take effect

    From August 2, the EU AI Office gained formal investigative and fining power over general-purpose AI providers, with Article 50 disclosure duties live and fines reaching €35M or 7% of global turnover, while Article 10's high-risk data-governance rules stay deferred to 2027. European Commission · 2026-08-02

    Any customer-facing generative feature reaching EU users needs an audited disclosure trail now, and the Article 10 deferral is a one-year runway to get data lineage in order, not a reason to deprioritize it.

  3. 03
    MCP's July 28 update drops server-side state

    The Model Context Protocol's 2026-07-28 specification removes server-side session state, aiming to cut the friction of deploying agentic workflows at enterprise scale. Model Context Protocol Blog · 2026-07-28

    A stateless protocol is easier to load-balance and govern, but any semantic-layer or catalog integration built against the old session model needs a compatibility check before the next MCP-based rollout.

  4. 04
    Databricks and OpenAI run agent production event

    Databricks and OpenAI are hosting a three-region virtual event this week on what separates a production agent from a demo, alongside new governed agent-building capabilities on the Databricks platform. Databricks · 2026-08-04

    A direct read on what two major vendors now call production-grade versus a demo is worth an hour before signing off on agent-fleet budget this quarter.

  5. 05
    Apache Polaris pulls 1.7.0 over licensing gap

    Polaris withdrew its 1.7.0 release candidate after a licensing gap surfaced in 44 of its staged jars, delaying the open lakehouse catalog's monthly release cadence. Apache Data Lakehouse Weekly · 2026-07-29

    A stumble in the release process of a catalog this central to Iceberg interoperability is a data point on Polaris's maturity — worth weighing against Unity Catalog or a cloud REST catalog.
