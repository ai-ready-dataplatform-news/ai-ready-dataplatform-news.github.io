AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Three in four enterprise AI agents sit dormant with live credentials

    Arrakis Security's 2026 Autonomous Agents Threat Report, published 21 October from 12 weeks of telemetry on thousands of agents in customer environments, found 76% of enabled agents had not run in 60 days yet kept working credentials and connections, about a quarter could be woken by an unsigned external webhook, and 99.4% of sensitive connections lacked verified data loss prevention. Hackread, on Arrakis Security's 2026 threat report · 2026-10-21

    Agent access to the platform needs a lifecycle like any service account: a PM putting agents on governed data has a case for expiry, owner attestation and usage-based revocation in the access model before the agent count grows. The figures are a security vendor's own telemetry, and the percentages use different denominators.

  2. 02
    Snowflake brings customer-held keys to its managed Iceberg tables

    Tri-Secret Secure, which combines a Snowflake key with a customer-managed key, became generally available on 30 October for Iceberg tables in Snowflake-managed storage on AWS and Azure, with its own registration functions, no waiting period before activation, no rekeying of existing data on key change and no private connectivity for key access. Snowflake release notes · 2026-10-30

    Customer-held keys were a common reason regulated teams kept sensitive tables in proprietary formats; with that gap closed, the open-format decision for those tables turns on the documented differences, especially that existing data is not rekeyed when a key changes.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Google's borderless Lakehouse caches across Glue, Unity and Horizon

    Google said on 15 October that bi-directional caching with AWS Glue, Databricks Unity Catalog and Snowflake Horizon, and Knowledge Catalog integration, are now generally available in its borderless Lakehouse, while letting Gemini agents reach Unity Catalog and Horizon data through catalog federation remains in preview. Google Cloud blog · 2026-10-15

    Agents reading across clouds through federated catalogs make the catalog, not the warehouse, the place where access and meaning are enforced. A platform roadmap should check that its policies and business definitions travel with the table, because the GA parts here cover the caching while the agent access is still preview.

  2. 02
    JPMorgan folds its data and analytics office into technology

    JPMorgan told staff on 1 July that chief data and analytics officer Teresa Heitsenrether retires at the end of 2026 and that CTO Scot Baldry adds the firmwide data and analytics title, aligning the function with global technology under the CIO and without the operating-committee seat she held. PYMNTS · 2026-07-01

    One of the largest data and AI programmes moving its data office under technology is a reference point for the operating-model question: when the data function reports into engineering, ownership of data products and of their business value needs an explicit home, or it drifts to whoever runs the pipelines.

video · InfoQ, Zhamak Dehghani · 2019-11-13
Data Mesh: Paradigm Shift in Data Platform Architecture

Most companies pour all their data into one central lake or warehouse and ask one central team to clean it for everyone, and that team becomes the bottleneck. This talk argues for flipping it: the teams that create the data, such as orders or payments, own it and publish it as a product, with a description, quality promises and an owner who answers for it. A shared self-serve platform gives those teams the storage, pipelines and access controls so they don't each rebuild them, and a light set of common rules keeps the products working together.

For the roadmap The talk is the source of the data-as-a-product idea that later work builds on, and it frames the platform PM's job as building the self-serve layer rather than owning every dataset. For a roadmap it asks one question to test against: which capabilities let a domain publish a governed, discoverable product without a ticket to the central team.
