AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Judge voids Pentagon's Anthropic risk label

    A federal judge ruled the Pentagon's 'supply chain risk' designation of Anthropic was unconstitutional retaliation and a due-process violation, and ordered it lifted. NPR · 2026-08-28

    Vendor-risk labels a federal agency pins on an AI supplier are now provably contestable in court, so procurement should treat one as a challengeable claim, not a final verdict, when scoring AI vendor risk.

  2. 02
    Genie One adds web search with citations

    Genie One can now search the public web for release notes, documentation and news to answer questions, and cites the external sources it used as links in its response. Databricks AI/BI and Genie One release notes · 2026-09-01

    Ungoverned public search inside a Unity Catalog-grounded assistant blurs the audit line between answers backed by governed data and ones pulled from the open web, so admins need a way to scope or log when Genie steps outside the catalog.

  3. 03
    Study finds 90% of leaked agent-skill secrets usable

    Testing 170,226 outputs from 17,022 skills on the SkillsMP marketplace, Wake Forest researchers found 89.6% of credentials leaked by LLM agent skills were immediately exploitable. Wake Forest News · 2026-08-26

    Any roadmap that lets agents install third-party skills needs a credential-scanning gate before a marketplace skill reaches production, not a policy document after the fact.

  4. 04
    KT rebuilds Woori Bank's chatbot on agent hand-offs

    KT won the contract to rebuild Woori Bank's chatbot and consultation-bot systems around its new Agent Connect layer, which carries context across channels and hands off between agents. Seoul Economic Daily · 2026-08-31

    A regulated bank betting customer service on context-preserving agent hand-offs is a live test of whether that architecture survives financial-services audit and continuity requirements.

  5. 05
    DoIT itemizes AI inference spend by model

    DoIT's Cloud Intelligence now itemizes inference spend by model across OpenRouter, xAI, Fireworks AI, Snowflake and Databricks alongside the rest of cloud costs. DoIT · 2026-08-31

    Per-model cost attribution across vendors closes a blind spot FinOps teams have been guessing around all year, and it is the kind of visibility a platform PM should be demanding on every AI billing line.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

video · AtScale Data-Driven Podcast (YouTube) · 2026
AI Needs Context: Semantic Layers, Metadata & Trust

A language model can write a database query, but it has no idea what your business means by 'active customer' or 'net revenue' unless someone tells it. A semantic layer is that telling: a governed map of what each term means, which tables hold it, and how it should be combined. Without the map, the model is guessing at a schema it has never really understood. With it, the model works from an agreed answer instead of inventing one.

For the roadmap The discussion cites research showing GPT-4 wrote correct SQL from a bare schema only 16.7% of the time, rising to 54.2% once the same database was wrapped in an ontology and mappings — evidence that a semantic and metadata layer belongs on the roadmap before agentic BI, not after, since accuracy roughly triples on identical data once context is added.
