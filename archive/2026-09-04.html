  1. 01
    Tealium Ships Configuration MCP for Governed Agent Access

    Tealium launched a Configuration MCP that lets MCP-compatible agents create and modify customer-data configurations in draft form pending human review, alongside a Developer Portal with versioned Platform APIs. Yahoo Finance · 2026-08-31

    A concrete draft-then-approve pattern for letting agents touch a governed customer-data platform without losing control — worth benchmarking against your own MCP governance model.

  2. 02
    AI Token Prices Cross Below $1 for First Time

    The LLM Token Expenditure Index fell to 97 cents per million tokens on September 1, its lowest level yet and less than half its summer peak, as cheaper open-weight models intensify price competition. Aroged · 2026-09-02

    Falling per-token prices won't shrink your AI bill on their own — agentic workloads multiply tokens per task, so budget on task volume, not sticker price.

  3. 03
    Nvidia Agrees to Buy Hugging Face for $12.9B

    Nvidia signed a definitive agreement on September 2 to acquire Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, folding the open-source model hub into its own AI stack, with the deal expected to close in the first half of 2027. TechCrunch · 2026-09-03

    A neutral model registry moving under a compute vendor is a vendor-risk signal — reassess dependency on Hugging Face-hosted models and weight portability into next year's platform roadmap.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

blog · Modern Data 101
AI-Ready Data vs. Analytics-Ready Data

Analytics-ready data answers the questions someone already thought to ask: clean columns, agreed definitions, a dashboard that renders correctly. AI-ready data has to answer questions nobody wrote down in advance — an agent might ask what a field means, how fresh it is, or handle an edge case no one anticipated. That means the context — definitions, lineage, constraints — has to travel attached to the data itself, not sit in a separate document a model can't reach.

For the roadmap Treat AI-ready as a distinct roadmap deliverable from BI-ready, not a side effect of good BI hygiene — budget separately for embedding definitions and context into the data layer itself, since that is the gap agents actually trip over.
