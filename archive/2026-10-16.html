AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    FSB finalises twelve sound practices for AI in finance

    The Financial Stability Board published its final Sound Practices for Responsible Adoption of AI on 15 October: twelve non-binding practices that scale human oversight with an agent's autonomy, require logging that lets firms reconstruct agent actions, and add guidance on third-party AI providers to Sound Practice 12. Forbes · 2026-10-15

    For a platform PM in financial services, reconstructable agent action logs and third-party AI provider oversight become the reference point national supervisors will translate, which shapes what the governed layer has to record before agents query it.

  2. 02
    41% of regulated firms have blocked an AI deployment over security

    A Fortanix survey of 300 AI leaders in banking, insurance, healthcare and government found 41% had completely blocked an AI deployment over security and 59% had delayed one, with regulatory compliance the top obstacle at 46%, while 61% still send sensitive data to public cloud AI services. Business Wire (Fortanix) · 2026-10-13

    It quantifies how often security review, not model quality, decides whether an AI use case ships, which strengthens the case for putting data classification and access policy ahead of the next agent pilot on the roadmap.

  3. 03
    PitchBook sells its data to agents through an MCP

    PitchBook launched an agentic platform assistant and a PitchBook Data MCP on 14 October, bringing its private-market intelligence into investors' own systems and AI agents rather than only its own interface. Business Wire (PitchBook) · 2026-10-14

    A financial data vendor packaging its licence as an agent-callable interface is a pricing and packaging precedent for anyone building a commercial data product, where the consumer is increasingly an agent, not a seat.

  4. 04
    Accenture buys UK customer intelligence firm Sagacity

    Accenture acquired London-based Sagacity on 13 October, adding more than 140 data, AI and customer intelligence specialists to Accenture Song's UK and Ireland business, targeting banks, utilities, retailers and telecoms; terms were not disclosed. Accenture Newsroom · 2026-10-13

    Customer-data specialists folding into a large consultancy shifts the build-versus-partner call for UK banks weighing outside help on customer data quality and AI-ready customer views.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Only 11% of insurers say their data is ready beyond pilots

    KPMG's Unlocking AI value in insurance survey found 44% of insurers place themselves in the top quartile for AI transformation, yet only 11% said their data foundations and governance were ready to take AI beyond pilots, citing fragmented data, unclear ownership and legacy systems. KPMG · 2026-09-29

    The gap between self-rated AI leadership and data foundations gives a foundation-phase platform a benchmark to argue that readiness, not adoption, is the honest early metric.

  2. 02
    Unstructured data survey finds maturity claims outrun AI results

    A Komprise survey of 500 technical decision makers found 68% describe their organisation as maturing or mature with AI but only 28% have quantified ROI, with most saying the bulk of their data is not ready for AI, and finding the right data and cleaning duplicates topping the pain points. GlobeNewswire (Komprise) · 2026-10-15

    It puts unstructured data discovery and deduplication on the readiness plan alongside the governed tables, since that is where respondents say AI projects stall.

  3. 03
    Snowflake lineage now reaches Cortex Search and model monitors

    Snowflake extended data lineage on 6 October to storage integrations, external tables, model monitors and Cortex Search services, tracing a search service back through processed tables to its source stage, though relationships are recorded only for objects created after the change. Snowflake release notes · 2026-10-06

    Lineage that runs from a retrieval service back to source files is what lets a governance team answer which documents a model could see, a test worth applying to any platform's RAG layer.

  4. 04
    BigQuery's AI.AGG summarises unstructured rows in SQL

    BigQuery made the AI.AGG function generally available on 13 October, letting a SQL query aggregate unstructured data semantically from a natural-language instruction, alongside general availability of a new Gemini model for its generative AI functions. Google Cloud release notes · 2026-10-13

    Semantic aggregation inside the warehouse moves some text analysis out of side pipelines and into governed SQL, which changes where quality and access controls for unstructured fields have to sit.

  5. 05
    dbt adds native Redshift data sharing reads and writes

    dbt Labs shipped native support for Amazon Redshift data sharing on 9 October, moving dbt-redshift to Redshift-native system views so cross-database writes, cross-project references and workload isolation work across datashares, behind a beta flag. dbt Labs · 2026-10-09

    Cross-project references over datashares let a producing domain publish governed models that a consuming domain builds on without copying, which bears on where modelling authority sits between central and domain teams.

When one team's data models get big, other teams start depending on them, and a change in one place can quietly break reports somewhere else. This course shows how to split one large project into several, each owned by a team, and connect them with clear rules. A contract promises the shape of a model so it cannot change by accident, a version lets a team change it without breaking the people still using the old one, and access groups decide which models other teams are allowed to build on. Together they turn a model into something other teams can rely on, like a product.

For the roadmap It is a concrete mechanism for the central-versus-domain modelling question: contracts and access groups let the platform team set the rules while domains own and publish their own governed models.
