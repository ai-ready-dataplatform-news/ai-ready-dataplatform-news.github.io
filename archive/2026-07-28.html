AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    China's agentic AI rules take effect

    China's Implementation Opinions on intelligent agents became enforceable on 15 July as the first dedicated regulatory framework for agentic AI, with the EU Digital Omnibus deadline landing in August. AI Governance Institute · 16 July 2026

    Agent access to your platform now needs logged inputs, explainable automated decisions and jurisdiction-aware policy — platform capabilities to fund, not per-product features to bolt on.

  2. 02
    Gartner: MCP alone won't carry agentic analytics

    Gartner's Andres Garcia-Rodeja told the 2026 Data and Analytics Summit that 60% of agentic analytics projects relying solely on MCP will fail by 2028 for want of a consistent semantic layer underneath. Strategy · July 2026

    The semantic layer stops being the BI team's choice and becomes a platform dependency you own, version and staff — worth pulling forward if it sits late on your roadmap.

  3. 03
    Only 6% have data ready to scale AI

    Across 10,000 businesses, 76% now report some measurable AI ROI and 34% are scaling AI, yet only 6% say their enterprise data is fully ready to support it at scale. Dun & Bradstreet AI Momentum Survey · July 2026

    That gap is the clearest business case your platform roadmap will get this quarter — measure readiness on your own estate before the shortfall gets filed as a model problem.

  4. 04
    Databricks deepens Azure bet as Fabric courts agents

    At the 2026 AI + Data Summit Databricks said it now runs its own core business operations on Azure Databricks, while Microsoft positions Fabric as the data platform for AI agents. BigDATAwire · 24 July 2026

    Consolidation is being sold on agent-readiness; press both vendors on open-format exit paths before an integration discount quietly settles your architecture.

  5. 05
    Iceberg turns to credentials and encryption

    The community is standardising vended-credential property names across engines and working through table-level encryption in Polaris, alongside staged creates in multi-table transactions. Apache Data Lakehouse Weekly · 16–23 July 2026

    Catalog, credential and encryption boundaries are hardening into contracts — decide now what your platform manages and what it passes through to engines.
