AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    MCP goes stateless, adds enterprise auth in major update

    The Model Context Protocol's July 28 spec release rebuilds the core as a stateless request/response protocol and stabilizes an Enterprise-Managed Authorization extension already being adopted by Anthropic, Microsoft and Okta. TechTarget · 2026-07-28

    Stateless servers can run behind standard load balancers on existing Kubernetes tooling — worth revisiting any MCP server design that assumed persistent sessions before the next agent rollout.

  2. 02
    EU AI Act's high-risk training-data rules take effect

    As of August 2, Article 10 of the EU AI Act requires providers of high-risk AI systems to document and govern training, validation and testing data — origin, labelling, cleaning and enrichment included — alongside Annex III conformity assessments. Responsible AI Labs · 2026-08-02

    Data-prep steps that used to be internal engineering choices are now regulated inputs with a documentation requirement — worth checking whether current lineage tooling can actually produce that trail on demand.

  3. 03
    Snowflake launches Cortex AI Gateway to govern agent fleets

    Snowflake's new Cortex AI Gateway, built on its Natoma Labs acquisition, gives enterprises a centralized control layer for AI agent identity, MCP governance, data-exfiltration prevention and cost tracking across more than 100 supported MCP servers. SiliconANGLE · 2026-07-28

    A platform vendor is now selling agent governance as a native gateway rather than leaving it to a third-party tool — worth comparing against whatever bespoke agent-access controls are already on the roadmap.

  4. 04
    Apache Polaris 1.7.0 tightens credential vending

    The August 2 Polaris 1.7.0 release adds opt-in idempotent createTable/updateTable calls, a stricter least-privilege credential-vending model with per-request IAM session policies, and a pass on orphan-file cleanup. Apache Polaris · 2026-08-02

    Tighter, request-scoped vended credentials close a real gap for any Iceberg catalog exposed to more callers as agentic tools start querying it directly — worth a version check before the next catalog upgrade window.

  5. 05
    Groundcover raises $100M to build observability for the AI era

    Groundcover closed a $100M Series C led by One Peak to expand its eBPF-based, zero-instrumentation observability platform, now serving 250+ customers including Fortune 5 enterprises, and to build out an agentic "Agent Mode" for production troubleshooting. Network World · 2026-07-29

    Fresh capital flowing into agentless observability tooling signals the buy case for monitoring AI-era pipelines is strengthening relative to building one in-house — worth a look before committing engineering time to a homegrown collector.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Databricks extends Unity Catalog to govern AI assets, not just tables

    Unity Catalog now registers, discovers, secures and audits models, MCP services, agents and skills through the same governance framework used for tables, with Unity AI Gateway enforcing contextual policy at runtime. Databricks · 2026-05-20

    A catalog bought to govern tables is being asked to govern agents too — worth checking whether the current catalog's policy model actually extends to non-tabular AI assets before agents multiply faster than the governance does.

  2. 02
    Looker ties its semantic layer to agent actions, not just dashboards

    At Next '26, Google announced Looker BI Agents that trigger downstream business actions grounded directly in Looker's LookML semantic layer and enterprise governance framework, plus a native MCP server exposing that layer to outside tools. Google Cloud Blog · 2026-04-22

    The semantic layer is becoming the thing agents act through, not just query — a reason to weigh metric-definition debt in the BI layer as an AI-readiness cost, not only a reporting-consistency one.

  3. 03
    Collibra scores AI trust as a business metric, not a dashboard extra

    Collibra's new Business Metrics dashboard in AI Command Center quantifies AI trust and risk concentration across agents, use cases and models in one comparable score, arguing trust scoring is becoming a governance dependency rather than a nice-to-have. Collibra · 2026-06-26

    A quantified trust score is a concrete artifact to demand from any governance vendor evaluation — a comparison point that's harder to dodge than a feature checklist.

  4. 04
    Deloitte: CDAOs gain power but structures lag AI ambition

    Deloitte's CDAO survey of 100 C-suite data and AI leaders at $1B+ revenue companies found 78% say AI has increased their decision-making power and 89% are actively evolving data and AI strategy, but most still lack a settled operating structure for it. Deloitte · 2026-03-03

    Influence is growing faster than the org chart is catching up — a case for naming who actually owns AI-readiness decisions before the next reorg gets forced by an incident rather than a plan.

  5. 05
    59% of enterprises run AI agents; 26% can govern them

    ServiceNow's Enterprise AI Maturity Index, surveying 4,500 executives across 19 countries, found the average maturity score rose 16 points to 51 as AI spending grew 110% — but governance capability trails deployment by a wide margin. ServiceNow · 2026-06-09

    A named gap between deployment and governance maturity is a sharper budget argument than a general readiness plea — useful when governance work is competing against the next agent feature for funding.

Data migration isn't the same job as building an AI-ready platform

Moving data to the cloud and making it usable by AI are two different jobs, even though they get bundled into one project. Migration answers "where does the data live now" — it copies tables from an old system to a new one. Readiness answers a harder question: can a model, a dashboard, and an application all trust the same number for "revenue" without three teams rebuilding the logic three different ways? That trust comes from a "data product" — a dataset with an owner, a contract describing what it means and how fresh it has to be, and rules about who can use it and how. Fix the contract once, in one place, and every downstream consumer inherits the fix instead of needing its own patch.

For the roadmap Migration budget and readiness budget are not interchangeable — a platform roadmap that funds the move but not the data-product layer on top of it will land data in the cloud that still can't be trusted by the next model, which is the actual ask most stakeholders meant when they asked for "AI-ready."
