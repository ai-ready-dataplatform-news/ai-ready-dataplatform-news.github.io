case study · Sphere Inc
Why enterprise RAG breaks: root causes from 23 engagements

A RAG system lets an AI look things up in your documents before it answers. Most failures are not about the AI at all. Documents get cut into pieces badly, so the answer is split across two pieces. The permission tags saying who may see a document are missing, so the system cannot tell what to hide. Nobody measures answer quality until users complain. Sphere's post-mortems across 23 engagements put chunking mismatch at 61%, missing permission metadata at 52% and no evaluation framework at 43%.

For the roadmap The top causes are data-platform properties, not model properties, which makes ingest-time permission metadata and a standing evaluation set roadmap items for the governed layer rather than something each AI team patches alone.
