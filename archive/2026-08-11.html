AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Inriver benchmark: manufacturers overrate AI readiness

    Inriver's inaugural Product Data Maturity Index, surveying 405 manufacturing and wholesale leaders, finds product data is far less ready for agentic commerce and AI search than executives believe. GlobeNewswire (Inriver) · 2026-08-05

    Self-reported AI-readiness scores are the wrong input for a roadmap — budget the audit before the agent pilot.

  2. 02
    BigQuery tunes itself for thousand-query-per-minute agents

    Google detailed BigQuery's history-based autonomous query optimizer and advanced runtime, built for agents running thousands of queries per minute rather than a few analysts a day. Google Cloud Blog · 2026-08-07

    Manual tuning playbooks stop scaling once agents are the query author — treat autonomous optimization as a platform requirement, not a nice-to-have.

  3. 03
    Akamai launches AI usage control and DLP for browsers

    Akamai rebranded LayerX to Workforce Protector, adding real-time visibility, AI usage governance, and data-loss prevention at the point where employees interact with AI tools in the browser. GlobeNewswire (Akamai) · 2026-08-05

    PII controls are moving to the browser edge, not just the pipeline — factor that into where the next governance budget goes.

  4. 04
    Airlock Digital adds policy control for AI agents

    Airlock Digital unveiled Agentic AI Control & Governance at Black Hat USA, giving security teams command- and session-level visibility and centralized policy management over trusted AI agents. GlobeNewswire (Airlock Digital) · 2026-08-04

    Endpoint-level agent policy is becoming a distinct control plane from data-layer governance — know which one a vendor pitch actually solves.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Gurucul adds shadow-AI detection to its data pipelines

    Gurucul previewed runtime detection for AI and added AI-assisted parsing and pipeline health monitoring to Data Optimizer, aimed at surfacing shadow AI and enforcing a cost-optimized, vendor-neutral data fabric. PR Newswire (Gurucul) · 2026-08-04

    Discovering unsanctioned AI use inside the pipeline is becoming table stakes for 'governed' — ask vendors how they detect it, not just how they classify PII.

Artificial Intelligence Governance

AI governance is the set of rules and checkpoints that keep an AI system honest before it ships: who has to approve it, what it's allowed to touch, and how anyone would notice if it drifted off course. This course walks through the actual mechanics rather than the theory — assessing how ready a team really is, writing the checklists and approval gates that get bolted onto a workflow, and rolling that same process out consistently across many teams instead of reinventing it each time.

For the roadmap Treat governance readiness assessments and approval gates as a roadmap deliverable, not a compliance afterthought — the EU AI Act deadline just made the absence of one visible.
