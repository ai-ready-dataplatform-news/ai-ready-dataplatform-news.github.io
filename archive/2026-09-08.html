AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Gartner: Only 22% Have Scaled AI Enterprise-Wide

    A Gartner survey of 1,303 organizations found just 22% have successfully scaled AI across multiple business units, even as 85% of functional leaders plan to increase 2026 AI spending. Gartner · 2026-09-01

    Budget pitches for platform-wide AI expansion should lead with the scaling gap, not the pilot wins — funding follows proof of cross-unit reuse, not one team's demo.

  2. 02
    Snowflake Ships External Lineage to General Availability

    External lineage reached general availability on September 3, using the OpenLineage framework to trace metadata from tools like dbt and Airflow into Snowflake's native lineage graph. Snowflake Documentation · 2026-09-03

    Governance teams can now show a single lineage graph spanning ingestion tools and the warehouse, closing a gap that made proving AI training-data provenance to auditors and model owners harder than it should be.

  3. 03
    S&P Global Completes Acquisition of DatacenterHawk

    S&P Global closed its purchase of datacenterHawk on September 1, folding proprietary intelligence on global data-center and AI infrastructure capacity into its data business. S&P Global · 2026-09-01

    As AI infrastructure scarcity becomes a real planning input, expect more data vendors to buy capacity intelligence outright — worth watching before locking in long-term compute commitments.

  4. 04
    Tenable and OpenAI Launch Screening for Rogue AI Tools

    Unveiled at OpenAI's Cyber Summit, the CyberAgents Exchange AI Inspector combines OpenAI's GPT cyber models with Tenable research to vet agents, skills, MCP servers and multi-agent playbooks before deployment. Tenable · 2026-09-03

    A pre-deployment security gate for MCP servers gives platform teams a concrete answer to 'how do we vet a community MCP server' instead of trusting a registry listing on faith.

  5. 05
    CSA Details EU AI Act Duties for High-Risk Systems

    New guidance lays out the data-governance, transparency and human-oversight duties now enforceable for high-risk AI systems, including the Article 50 content-labeling rules that took effect August 2. Cloud Security Alliance · 2026-09-03

    If a platform feeds a high-risk EU use case, training-data provenance and disclosure metadata are now audit items, not nice-to-haves — budget for the paperwork alongside the pipeline work.

The Semantic Layer Is AI's Last-Mile Problem

An AI agent doesn't usually fail because the model is weak — it fails because nobody told it what your data actually means. A semantic layer is a plain-language dictionary that says 'revenue means this column, defined this way, filtered like this,' so an agent doesn't have to guess. Without it, two AI queries about 'active customers' can quietly return two different answers. Building one is like properly onboarding a new hire instead of dropping them into the business with no context — you explain what the terms mean before you ask them to act on anything.

For the roadmap If agent rollouts are stalling in pilot, the fix is rarely a better model — it's paying down semantic-layer debt first, since that's what most last-mile AI failures trace back to on the roadmap.
