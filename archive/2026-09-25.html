AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Open-source OpenObserve reaches v1.0 with AI observability

    OpenObserve's 1.0 release folds agent tracing, LLM monitoring and session evaluation into the same open-source platform that already covers logs, metrics, traces and real-user monitoring, pitched as a Datadog/Splunk alternative. BusinessWire · 2026-09-22

    A credible open-source entrant at the observability layer we buy from Monte Carlo is a genuine build-vs-buy data point, even before it matches our SLA and incident-response depth.

  2. 02
    Finosec launches an AI Governance module for banks

    The module gives a bank one inventory of where AI is in use across vendors and systems, scores the risk of each, and produces vendor due-diligence questionnaires and board-level reporting. FF News · 2026-09-21

    A packaged AI-vendor-risk register is the thing a regulated platform team usually ends up building by hand — worth benchmarking our own vendor inventory against what a dedicated tool now ships as standard.

  3. 03
    EU draft would let AI training skip GDPR consent

    A Council compromise text under the Digital Omnibus, criticised by privacy group noyb, would let personal data be processed to develop and operate AI under GDPR's 'legitimate interest' basis rather than requiring consent. The Register · 2026-09-22

    If this lands, the consent basis we've built PII handling around for anything touching model training shifts — worth flagging to legal now, ahead of the Council text firming up.

An agent wants governed, well-defined context it can query on demand and act on without anyone checking first. Most data platforms were only ever built for the first job. This course walks through what actually changes in modelling, ownership and architecture when the second job becomes a real requirement rather than an afterthought bolted onto the existing warehouse.
