case study · Intellias · 2026-06
Turning fragmented data into trust and revenue: GBG's platform rebuild

Most companies have plenty of data but not much of it is usable — it's scattered across systems, defined differently in each one, and hard to trust. 'Data capability modelling' is a way of mapping, before you build anything, which specific business outcomes you want and which data capabilities are actually missing to reach them, instead of modernizing infrastructure first and hoping usefulness follows. GBG, an identity and location-data company, used this approach and found its real problem wasn't a lack of data but the lack of one trusted, easily queried view across it.

For the roadmap Data capability modelling is a sequencing tool: run the gap assessment before committing to a platform architecture, and put 'can this data become a product' on the roadmap as its own line rather than assuming migration delivers it for free.
