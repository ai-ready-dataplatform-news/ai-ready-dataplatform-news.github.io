AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Sapiens launches AI-native autonomous insurance platform

    SapiensAIP unifies underwriting, policy, billing and claims into one agentic platform, built on a foundation layer drawn from Sapiens' own insurance ontology. Insurance Edge · 2026-09-15

    A core-admin vendor packaging governed context as a product raises the bar for what 'AI-ready' means in regulated insurance data, and what a platform roadmap now has to match.

  2. 02
    OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Financial Services

    GPT-6 Astra ships bundled with PitchBook, LSEG and Crunchbase data, plus shared access to Capital IQ and Factiva, aimed at the research work junior bankers do today. CNBC · 2026-09-10

    A model vendor is now also a data distributor: buy-vs-build and vendor-risk calls for research data have to weigh a single AI subscription against a stack of licensed feeds.

  3. 03
    Snowflake previews reading secrets from cloud vaults

    Queries can now read credentials straight from AWS Secrets Manager, Azure Key Vault or Google Cloud Secret Manager via workload identity federation, instead of holding a duplicate copy. Snowflake Documentation · 2026-09-10

    One fewer place secrets sit unmanaged is one fewer finding in the next access review, a small governance pattern worth copying into any platform's own credential story.

  4. 04
    Streaming landscape report adds sovereignty to vendor risk

    Citing June's government-ordered suspension of a major AI vendor's access for foreign users, the Q3 data streaming landscape report argues every platform dependency now carries a sovereignty question. Kai Waehner · 2026-09-07

    Vendor risk assessment needs a sovereignty column alongside cost and lock-in, not just an uptime SLA, before the next platform contract is signed.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Databricks ships a native FILE column type

    FILE, now in beta, stores documents, images, audio and video natively inside Delta tables, with vector and full-text indexes for direct search and retrieval. Databricks Blog · 2026-08-10

    Unstructured data stops being a side pipeline and becomes a governed column: the same lineage and access controls that cover a gold table now have to extend to what's inside it.

  2. 02
    Alation expands AIOS with six governance products

    The six new components, including AI Governance, Ontologies and Governed Collections, target a gap Alation cites: 78% of C-suite leaders lack confidence they'd pass an AI governance audit within 90 days. GlobeNewswire · 2026-09-17

    That 78% figure is a useful number to bring into a board conversation about what a foundation-phase platform should be judged on, rather than adoption alone.
