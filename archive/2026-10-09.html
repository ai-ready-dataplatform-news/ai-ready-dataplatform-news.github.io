AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Cloudflare Basin reaches general availability on Iceberg

    Cloudflare renamed its data platform Basin and made it generally available: pipelines, an Iceberg catalog and a SQL engine over R2 storage, with open tables that Snowflake, Spark and DuckDB can query and no transfer fees across clouds. Cloudflare blog · 2026-10-02

    A serverless Iceberg stack with zero egress gives a platform PM a concrete low-cost option to price against for edge and event data before committing it to the warehouse.

  2. 02
    Snowflake Horizon Iceberg tables now reach S3-compatible storage

    A private preview lets Horizon-managed Iceberg tables sit on S3-compatible stores such as MinIO, Ceph, Wasabi and Cloudflare R2, readable and writable from any compatible engine, aimed at residency, sovereign-cloud and on-premises needs. Snowflake engineering blog · 2026-10-05

    Where residency rules keep data off public cloud storage, the catalog-versus-storage split now decides whether one governed Iceberg layer can span on-premises and cloud.

  3. 03
    Dynatrace closes its Arize acquisition for AI observability

    Dynatrace completed the $915 million deal on 1 October; Arize keeps supporting open-source Phoenix and the enterprise AX platform while its evaluation tooling is folded into Dynatrace over time. Engineering.com · 2026-10-01

    Update you saw this on 2026-09-19 The deal was announced in August and has now closed, on 1 October.

    An independent AI evaluation vendor leaving the market is a vendor-risk input for any team that standardised on Arize or Phoenix for model-output monitoring.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Only 6% of firms say enterprise data is fully AI-ready

    Dun & Bradstreet's survey of 10,000 businesses in 32 countries finds about three-quarters see measurable AI return, yet only 6% call their data fully ready, 36% mostly ready and 47% partially ready, with access, privacy and quality the main obstacles. Dun & Bradstreet · 2026-07-28

    A vendor-run but large-sample yardstick for the readiness gap, useful as the baseline when a platform PM argues for governed-access work ahead of more models.

  2. 02
    BigID AgentIQ runs data security and compliance from a prompt

    BigID's AgentIQ lets customers query their data landscape, investigate access violations, rank findings by sensitivity and exposure, and trigger remediation such as revoking access, from a prompt or from Claude, Copilot, GPT or Gemini. Unite.AI · 2026-09-21

    Agent-driven remediation on top of data discovery shifts governed-clean work from manual review towards continuous enforcement, which changes how a roadmap sizes the stewardship team.
