  1. 01
    ICO opens call for evidence on agentic AI

    The UK Information Commissioner's Office opened a six-week call for evidence on 8 October, closing 20 November, on how data protection law applies to AI agents, covering security, transparency, accountability, automated decisions and lawful data use. LexisNexis · 2026-10-08

    A platform PM weighing agent access to governed data has a dated consultation to feed, and a signal that agent autonomy will not excuse weak lineage or access control.

    ICO secures data protection changes from ten AI developers

    The ICO's report says Amazon, Anthropic, Apple, Cohere, DeepSeek, Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI and Stability AI have made or committed to changes, and sets regulatory positions on special category data and whether models themselves can contain personal data. The Next Web · 2026-10-08

    Where a team fine-tunes or grounds models on customer data, the regulator's stance on personal data inside models is now a documented input to retention and consent design.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

blog · Data Mesh Learning
Data Product vs. Data Contract: What's the Difference?

A data product is the thing people actually use: a dataset packaged with an owner, a purpose and a way to reach it. A data contract is the written promise about that thing: what columns it has, how fresh it will be, how good it is, and how changes get announced. A product can offer several ways in, called output ports, and each one should carry its own promise. Mixing the two up is how teams end up with a pile of agreements and nothing anyone owns.

For the roadmap It gives a platform PM the vocabulary to decide which datasets earn a product owner and which only need a contract on their output, which sizes the stewardship work on a roadmap.
