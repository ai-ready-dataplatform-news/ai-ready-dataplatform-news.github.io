    Snowflake now quarantines bad rows at load time

    Error logging and CHECK constraint enforcement for COPY INTO and Snowpipe reached general availability on 19 November. With error logging on and ON_ERROR set to CONTINUE, rows that fail a type conversion or break a business rule, such as a negative quantity, are written to an error table with the reason they failed, while the valid rows still load. The defaults differ, though: SKIP_FILE for Snowpipe and ABORT_STATEMENT for COPY INTO. Snowflake engineering blog and release notes · 2026-11-19

    This puts a basic quality gate inside the warehouse's load path, so a buy-versus-build decision on data quality now starts from what the platform already rejects and records. The question to settle is whether rule definitions live in table constraints or in the transformation framework, because keeping both creates two sources of truth.

How to lead data-centric product teams

On most product teams the customer sits outside the building. On a data team, many of the customers are other engineers and analysts, and what they receive is a table, an interface or a model rather than a screen. The product manager's job there is to understand those interfaces well enough to describe them clearly, to keep asking whether each piece of work serves someone's real need, and to be honest about the hardest problem: whether the ground truth the data is checked against can actually be trusted.

For the roadmap This frames the platform PM role as interface ownership plus customer empathy. In practice that means writing the contract and the documentation for a governed dataset as part of the product, and testing roadmap items against a named consumer's need rather than against what the engineering team finds interesting.
