AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Fox names Databricks its platform of choice for data and agents

    Fox Corporation signed a multi-year strategic partnership on 13 October naming Databricks its platform of choice for data, AI applications and agents, using Lakebase, Genie Code, Unity Gateway and Genie Ontology, with a Sports AI agent built on Lakebase. Databricks · 2026-10-13

    A large media operator consolidating agents, governance and the context layer on one vendor gives a platform PM a reference point for the single-platform versus best-of-breed call on the AI roadmap.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Teradata adds a vendor-neutral context engine above existing data

    Teradata announced on 22 September a Tera Context Engine that sits above existing databases, catalogs and pipelines and maps metadata, lineage and business meaning in a context graph without moving data, due for general availability by the end of the year. TechTarget · 2026-09-22

    A context layer that spans vendors rather than living inside one platform puts a build-versus-buy question on where agent-facing business definitions are owned.

  2. 02
    Governance and AI teams are sharing responsibility for controlling agents

    Reporting from Dataversity's AI Governance Online conference describes agents needing well-managed metadata and permission checks that continue while a job runs, with human review reserved for consequential changes such as edits to sensitive master data, and auditable records of agent actions. TechTarget · 2026-10-01

    It frames continuous permission checks and audit trails as part of the governed layer itself, which shapes how access policy is scoped when agents join analysts as consumers.

  3. 03
    Defence suppliers publish validated evidence instead of answering data calls

    A Databricks post proposes a federated exchange in which suppliers keep governing source systems and publish validated cost and status evidence to named recipients over open sharing protocols, replacing manual data calls and spreadsheets across a base of more than 200,000 suppliers. Databricks · 2026-10-09

    It describes a data product as validated evidence with named recipients rather than a copy of source data, a pattern for any partner-facing data supply.

podcast · CDO Magazine
Data Products at Scale: How Henkel Aligns Ownership, Governance and Culture

A data product is data packaged the way a good product is: it has an owner, a clear purpose, a promise about quality, and people who rely on it. Henkel's data and analytics director describes building these around business areas such as finance, supply chain and sales, so each one has someone accountable. Governance is written into the product through contracts and transparency rather than added at the end. The hardest part she names is people, not technology: getting teams to reuse a shared product instead of building their own copy.

For the roadmap It gives a worked example of domain-aligned ownership with contracts as the governance mechanism, useful when deciding whether modelling authority sits with a central team or the consuming domains.
