AI news for Enterprise Data Platform Product Management

  1. 01
    Databricks adds explicit DENY rules to Unity Catalog ABAC

    Beta DENY policies let a catalog or schema explicitly block the MANAGE ACCESS CONTROL privilege, overriding any grant, including for object owners. Databricks · 2026-09-08

    Closes the gap where a table owner could reassign access outside policy — a real lever for keeping a governed gold layer's controls from drifting as more consumers attach to it.

  2. 02
    Databricks ships automatic change data feed for Delta and Iceberg v3

    Auto CDF computes row-level changes at query time from row tracking, without manually enabling CDF per table, cutting MERGE and UPDATE time by about 15% on tables that are queried for changes. Databricks · 2026-09-10

    Removes a manual step teams reliably forget to apply consistently across tables — one less place a change-tracking gap can hide inside a medallion pipeline.

  3. 03
    Databricks enforces explicit entitlement grants across every workspace

    New workspace entitlement control behaviour is now enforced everywhere, requiring admins to explicitly grant entitlements when adding principals rather than inheriting them by default. Databricks · 2026-09-14

    A default-access assumption a few onboarding scripts likely rely on just stopped being safe — worth checking before the next cohort of users lands on the platform.
