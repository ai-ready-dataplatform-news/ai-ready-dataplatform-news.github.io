AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Publicis closes LiveRamp deal under FTC data firewalls

    Publicis Groupe completed its roughly $2.2 billion all-cash purchase of LiveRamp on 2 November, keeping it as an independent company under CEO Scott Howe; under a proposed FTC order it must give LiveRamp's standalone products fair, non-discriminatory access for five years and keep firewalls so it cannot use LiveRamp customers' data. Adweek · 2026-11-02

    When a data-collaboration network is bought by a party that competes with some of its users, the neutrality promise moves into a regulator's order, so a data product team that shares or licenses data through a third-party network now has to judge the owner's incentives as well as the technology.

  2. 02
    Databricks makes Genie an autonomous agent with spending caps

    On 11 November Databricks relaunched Genie as an agent that runs long jobs in the background on its own compute and asks for input when it needs it, with Unity Gateway applying existing permissions, budgets, spending caps and full action tracing; Genie and Genie Ontology are listed as generally available, while the always-on mode and Genie Search are in preview and beta. Databricks newsroom · 2026-11-11

    Once an agent acts unattended on governed tables, the platform's permission and budget controls are the only thing standing between it and the gold layer, so a team piloting Genie should decide which datasets an autonomous job may touch, and who owns its spend, before switching the always-on mode on.

  3. 03
    Snowflake's AI gateway adds team budgets and per-user quotas

    Snowflake's 3 November release note makes Cortex AI Gateway inference, observability and cost management generally available: one endpoint taking OpenAI and Anthropic request formats, request-level traces with OpenTelemetry export, and usage reporting with team budgets and per-user quotas; tools for the gateway and trace analytics stay in preview. Snowflake release notes · 2026-11-03

    Per-user quotas and team budgets on model calls make AI spend something that can be charged back the way warehouse compute is, which gives a platform team a concrete option to weigh before building its own model-routing and cost-attribution layer.

  4. 04
    IBM and Microsoft launch an agent platform, finance first

    IBM and Microsoft announced Imagine Enterprise on 10 November, a platform for building and overseeing industry-specific agents that reports latency, cost, token use and run history and sets which actions an agent may take on its own and which need human approval; it launches first in financial services, insurance and retail. IBM Newsroom · 2026-11-10

    Large suppliers are now packaging agent approval rules for regulated firms, so a financial-services data platform team should be clear which approvals belong in the data layer, through entitlements and lineage, and which it is willing to leave to an application vendor's control panel.

  5. 05
    AI returns rise while readiness for risk falls to 24%

    Kyndryl's third Readiness Report, released 10 November from a survey of 3,700 leaders in 21 countries, finds 64% report positive returns on AI, up from 54% in 2025, while average readiness for external risks fell seven points to 24%; firms that redesigned roles and ran formal change management reported positive returns at 69%, against 53% for the rest. Kyndryl · 2026-11-10

    A 16-point gap between firms that redesigned roles and those that did not is a number to bring to a platform business case, because it says the operating-model work around a dataset (ownership, stewardship, who acts on it) shows up in returns as much as the technology does.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Hyland open-sources a hub that keeps document permissions for AI

    Hyland released OpenArch on 10 November under the Apache 2.0 licence: a hub that indexes content, metadata and permissions from many document repositories for AI search and retrieval, storing only the AI-ready copy and its permissions while the originals and their governance stay in the source systems. Hyland via PR Newswire · 2026-11-10

    Unstructured documents usually reach a model through a side pipeline that drops the source system's access rules; a hub that carries those rules into retrieval is a pattern worth testing before building document RAG on top of a lakehouse that only governs tables.

  2. 02
    Confluent adds a query layer to serve live context to agents

    Confluent, now part of IBM, introduced a Streamhouse architecture on 4 November that adds Query as a fifth pillar of its streaming platform, combining Tableflow's open-table output for lakehouses with a Real-Time Context Engine that serves business context to agents over MCP, and low-latency Lightning Tables. Confluent · 2026-11-04

    If agents read current state from the stream while dashboards read the gold layer, the two can disagree about the same customer, so a platform team running Kafka beside a medallion lakehouse needs one set of definitions, and one owner, for both paths.

video · Atlan Re:Govern (Fabien Thiaucourt and Vivek Radhakrishnan, Mastercard)
How Mastercard is Building AI-Ready Data Products

A data product is a dataset that a team looks after the way a company looks after something it sells: it has an owner, a clear description of what it means, and promises about quality. Making one 'AI-ready' means writing that meaning down in a form a model can read too, not just a person, so a model asking about it gets the same answer an analyst would. In this 22-minute talk, two Mastercard governance leads argue that the meaning has to be added while the product is built, not catalogued afterwards, and that engineers and the governance team have to own the programme together.

For the roadmap For a platform still in its foundation phase, the talk is a reference for one live decision: whether business definitions are written into each governed dataset as it is onboarded, or left for a central catalogue team to add later.
