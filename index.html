AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Microsoft puts PostgreSQL inside Fabric with HorizonDB

    At Ignite on 17 November Microsoft introduced Fabric HorizonDB in preview, bringing PostgreSQL into Fabric so that a single query can combine live operational data with analytical data in OneLake and HorizonDB tables copy into Fabric without separate pipelines, and listed Database Hub in Fabric as generally available with SQL and PostgreSQL database agents in preview. Microsoft Ignite 2026 Book of News · 2026-11-17

    When operational rows and analytical tables sit behind one query, the governance boundary of a medallion platform gets harder to hold: a platform PM opening agentic access has to decide whether agents may read live operational data that never passed through the curated gold layer.

  2. 02
    Claude Data goes generally available over company warehouses

    Anthropic made Claude Dashboards generally available on 10 November under a new name, Claude Data, so teams on paid plans can query connected company data and build live dashboards from sources including Amazon Redshift, BigQuery and ClickHouse, with Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets entering beta alongside it. The New Stack · 2026-11-10

    A general-purpose assistant now queries the warehouse directly, next to platform-native agents such as Genie, so a gold layer serving conversational access needs one set of metric definitions and access rules that every assistant reads, or two assistants will give two answers to the same question.

  3. 03
    ClickHouse adds Postgres, observability and a SIEM at once

    On 9 November ClickHouse made Managed Postgres generally available in ClickHouse Cloud, opened ClickHouse Observability in public beta, put an agentic security product built on its RunReveal acquisition into private preview, introduced an open-source AI gateway through Langfuse, and announced the acquisition of Xata. iTWire · 2026-11-09

    Analytical engines are turning into whole-stack platforms, so the buy-versus-build question for operational Postgres, telemetry and LLM tracing now includes the analytics vendor, and the lock-in of each extra workload belongs in the concentration-risk case rather than the feature list.

  4. 04
    Databricks commits $3 billion to Europe and the Middle East

    Databricks announced from London on 12 November plans to invest approximately $3 billion across Europe and the Middle East over three years, its largest regional investment outside North America, expecting its EMEA workforce to grow by about 50% and aiming to train 500,000 people in data and AI across EMEA by 2030; the release does not split the spend by region. BigDATAwire · 2026-11-12

    For a regulated UK buyer this is a vendor-commitment signal rather than a residency promise, since the announcement does not tie the money to new regions, so a renewal or concentration-risk paper can cite it for support depth and local hiring, not for where data will live.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Snowflake semantic views can now compute at a different grain

    Snowflake added level-of-detail expressions to semantic views in preview on 2 November, letting facts, dimensions and metrics be calculated at a grain different from the query's, defined in SQL or in the YAML specification; LOD metrics work in SQL queries and Semantic Studio, but Cortex Analyst and Cortex Agents do not use them yet. Snowflake release notes · 2026-11-02

    It shows the analytical-to-agentic gap in a single release note: a metric can now be defined correctly for the BI path while the platform's own agents cannot read it, so a semantic layer serving both consumers needs a check of which definitions each path actually uses.

  2. 02
    Only 7% have most unstructured file data ready for AI

    A Komprise survey of 500 technical decision-makers at organisations with at least 1,000 employees, fielded in September, found only 7% have cleaned, classified and prepared 70% or more of their unstructured file data for AI, 57% say half or less is ready and 13% cannot say, while only 4% have built schema for more than 70% of it in lakehouse-accessible form; Komprise sells software for this work. Blocks and Files · 2026-10-19

    Most readiness figures describe tables; this one describes the documents beside them, which is exactly where a table-first governed estate has no coverage, and it gives a baseline for deciding whether documents enter the governed platform or stay a side pipeline.

podcast · The Analytics Power Hour, episode 220, with Austin Byrne of Canva
Product Management for Data Products and Data Platforms

A usual product manager builds something people use directly, like an app, and can watch whether they come back to it. A data product manager builds things other people use to make their own things: a trusted table, a metric, a platform that teams put dashboards and models on. The users are mostly colleagues, the product is invisible when it works, and its value shows up later in someone else's decision. So the craft is the same, understanding the user, choosing what not to build and proving value, but it is applied to something whose value is indirect.

For the roadmap The episode compares ordinary product management with data product and platform product management, with Canva's group product lead for data, which helps make the case that a foundation-phase platform is judged on what downstream teams can now do rather than on raw usage; on a roadmap that means naming one downstream outcome per governed dataset, such as time to insight or analysis no longer re-derived, as its headline measure.
