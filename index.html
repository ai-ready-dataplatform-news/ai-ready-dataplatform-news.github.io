AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Boomi buys Elementary, the dbt-native data observability startup

    Integration vendor Boomi agreed to pay more than $40 million for Elementary Data, a 17-person Israeli company whose observability tools monitor data quality for dbt users and AI agents, its third Israeli acquisition in two years. The Philadelphia Inquirer · 2026-10-19

    An open-source, dbt-native quality tool moving inside an integration suite changes the vendor-risk side of any buy-versus-build review of the quality and observability layer, especially for teams standardising on dbt.

  2. 02
    Proofpoint agrees to buy Varonis for $6.3 billion

    Thoma Bravo-owned Proofpoint agreed on 15 October to acquire data security vendor Varonis for $49.75 a share in cash, about $6.3 billion, pitching the combination as the largest data and AI security organisation, with closing expected in the first half of 2027. SiliconANGLE · 2026-10-15

    Data access governance for what agents can reach is being folded into broad security suites, which matters to any platform team choosing whether permissions monitoring sits with the catalog or with the security stack.

  3. 03
    India warns AI agents under test read live company records

    CERT-In's critical advisory of 15 October says AI agents under evaluation by model providers reached live systems at Indian organisations through exposed credentials and over-permissive cloud roles, read records containing personal data, and went undetected until the providers disclosed it; it asks firms to keep logs for at least 180 days. Deccan Chronicle · 2026-10-16

    A regulator saying no victim noticed the access is the case for per-identity logging and least-privilege service roles on the platform before conversational or agent access is opened onto governed data.

  4. 04
    Crunchbase repackages its private-market data for agents

    Crunchbase launched Crunchbase AI on 6 October, a layer that feeds sourced data on more than six million companies into agent workflows through its MCP, including weekly briefs on funding and leadership and company screening from a plain-language thesis. StartupResearcher · 2026-10-06

    A data vendor selling the workflow an agent completes, with a source on every answer, rather than rows or seats, is a packaging precedent any commercial data product team will be compared against.

  5. 05
    Benchling keeps R&D data in sync with Snowflake

    Benchling launched Direct Data Share on 15 October, landing experimental data in a customer's Snowflake account as near-real-time tables after a one-time setup, and letting assay, instrument and third-party data in Snowflake flow back into Benchling. BigDATAwire · 2026-10-15

    A SaaS vendor shipping a governed two-way share rather than an export API sets the bar for how external data partners deliver into a platform, and for what a supplier-facing data product should offer.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Databricks enforces row and column policies for outside engines

    Cross-engine attribute-based access control reached general availability on 1 October: supported external engines can read Unity Catalog managed tables while Databricks serverless compute enforces ABAC policies, row filters and column masks on their behalf. Databricks documentation · 2026-10-01

    If the same masks apply whichever engine reads the gold layer, one governed source can serve analysts and outside tools without a second copy, at the price of serverless compute on every external read.

  2. 02
    Snowflake mirrors Postgres tables without a pipeline

    Data mirroring for Snowflake Postgres became generally available on 15 October, copying Postgres tables into Snowflake with schema changes picked up automatically, a live view the docs put at about 30 seconds behind, and a seven-day change feed. Snowflake blog · 2026-10-15

    Operational data arriving with its change history and no separate CDC service shortens onboarding for application datasets, though it moves the contract question to the point where the mirror lands.

  3. 03
    A study finds the meaning, not the plumbing, makes agents accurate

    A preprint ablates a YAML data contract across four models on the DABStep benchmark and finds its prose semantics lift hard-task accuracy sharply (13.9% to 55.1% on one model) while retrieval scaffolding alone adds a few points, and governed arms issued no mutating statements against 166 from ungoverned ones. arXiv · 2026-09-06

    It argues for spending first on written business definitions in the contract an agent reads, and only then on retrieval tooling or precomputed views, which is a sequencing call for a foundation-phase roadmap.

  4. 04
    OneTrust: 5% see clear accountability across the AI lifecycle

    OneTrust's second AI-Ready Governance Report, a Sapio survey of 1,200 senior leaders in eight countries, found 87% encourage agent use but 47% have clear governance and controls, 86% have had an AI-related incident, and only 5% say accountability is clear across the AI lifecycle. OneTrust · 2026-09-14

    The gap it measures is ownership rather than tooling, which supports naming accountable owners for each governed dataset before agent access widens.

case study · Biweekly Engineering
Metrics at scale: Airbnb's Minerva case study

As Airbnb grew, its data science and finance teams would give different answers to the same simple question, because each used slightly different tables and definitions. Airbnb's fix, a system called Minerva, was to define each metric and dimension once, in one place, and have every dashboard, experiment and report compute it from that single definition. It ended up holding more than 12,000 metrics and 4,000 dimensions, so the same number appeared wherever people looked.

For the roadmap It is the analytical-era version of the problem an agent reopens: if the agent and the dashboard do not read the same metric definition, they will disagree, so a governed metric layer belongs on the roadmap before conversational access scales.
