AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Morningstar rebuilds Direct around three research agents

    Morningstar launched Direct AI worldwide on 7 October: Product Development, Distribution and Manager Research agents that draw on its data, ratings and the work of more than 1,100 analysts, behind a new browser interface. InvestmentNews · 2026-10-07

    A licensed financial data set is being repackaged as agents rather than feeds, which gives a data product PM a live example of where the commercial packaging of data is heading.

  2. 02
    Plaid's fall release adds transformer credit and fraud models

    Plaid says LendScore Arc, its first transformer-based credit risk score, lifted subprime approvals 20% in early testing, and its Signal 4 payment-risk model caught 126% more risky dollars than the previous one. Plaid · 2026-10-07

    A data network selling scored outputs instead of raw account data shows the product move a platform PM weighing a data-as-a-product offer would be benchmarked against; the figures are Plaid's own early tests.

  3. 03
    OneTrust adds CORIE to enforce policy on AI agent calls

    CORIE reads existing privacy, consent, risk and data policies and allows, blocks or escalates each agent tool call, logging the agent, purpose, outcome and governing policy; OneTrust's own report puts clear agent governance at 47% of organisations. OneTrust · 2026-09-30

    Where agents will query a governed gold layer, per-call policy enforcement with an audit record is a requirement to put in the access-control design, not a later add-on.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    Dell adds a semantic layer and knowledge graph for agents

    Dell announced a Unified Semantic Layer, an Enterprise Knowledge Graph built from metadata, lineage and query history, and Knowledge Agents with set quality thresholds and spending limits; SiliconANGLE reports the three are due in the first half of 2027. Network World · 2026-10-07

    A storage vendor moving into the semantic layer shows business definitions becoming a shared dependency of every agent, which strengthens the case for agreeing metric definitions in the governed layer first.

  2. 02
    Precisely opens AI Studio with data-quality skills for agents

    AI Studio, launched 6 October with a 30-day trial, packages apps, agents and downloadable skills for data quality and enrichment, pre-connected via MCP and usable from Claude, Copilot and ChatGPT. BigDATAwire · 2026-10-06

    Quality checks delivered as agent skills raise the build-versus-buy question for the cleaning step that sits between a gold layer and a model.

case study · GEICO Tech Blog
The Path to a Successful Lakehouse: Lessons and the AI-Powered Future

A data contract is a written promise between the team that makes a dataset and the teams that use it: what it contains, how fresh it will be, and how changes get announced. GEICO found the hard part was people, not tools: source teams saw contracts as paperwork until they were told the contract was a shared responsibility and they kept ownership. Checks that run automatically when data arrives make the promise cheap to keep. Start with dull, repeatable work first, because that is how trust gets built.

For the roadmap It shows how to introduce contracts without a central mandate, which sizes the change-management work on a governed-dataset roadmap more honestly than the tooling does.
