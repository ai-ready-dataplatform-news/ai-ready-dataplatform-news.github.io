AI news for Data Products and Data Platforms

  1. 01
    Snowflake shares Iceberg tables with non-Snowflake engines

    Snowflake's release note of 2 November moves Open Data Sharing to general availability: providers can share live, read-only Snowflake-managed Iceberg tables with consumers who have no Snowflake account, who read them from their own engine through the standard Iceberg REST Catalog API with short-lived tokens; standard Snowflake tables and externally managed Iceberg tables are not shareable this way. Data Studios, on Snowflake's 2 November release note · 2026-11-02

    A data product team selling or exchanging data no longer has to make buyers open a Snowflake account or run export jobs, which changes the packaging decision for partner-facing data supply: the open table format becomes the delivery contract, and the consumer's engine stops being the provider's problem.

  2. 02
    FCA finds a coding defect starved a bank's crime control

    The FCA's Wholesale Banks Supervision Insights 3, published 2 November, describes data that was never ingested into a financial crime control because of a coding defect and went unnoticed for an extended period, and expects firms to check source-data lineage and completeness rather than trust downstream monitoring; on AI it found incomplete inventories, weak kill-switch arrangements and enterprise search making misclassified data easier to find. Financial Conduct Authority · 2026-11-02

    A regulator now names completeness checks at ingestion, not dashboards downstream, as the control it expects, so a platform PM in financial services has a supervisory reference for funding source-to-control reconciliation and for gating enterprise AI search on classification quality.

  3. 03
    Unity Catalog can now keep managed tables in OneLake

    Azure Databricks' 2 November release notes put OneLake external locations into Public Preview: a OneLake location can serve as a Unity Catalog catalog's managed storage, holding managed tables, volumes, materialized views and streaming tables in Microsoft OneLake while Unity Catalog keeps governing access, enabled by workspace admins from the Previews page. Azure Databricks release notes · 2026-11-02

    For an estate running Databricks for engineering and Power BI for consumption, the question of whether gold data has to be copied into Fabric gets a preview answer of no; a platform PM can test one governed catalog in OneLake before committing to a mirroring pipeline, keeping in mind it is not yet production-supported.

AI-ready vs analytical-ready data

  1. 01
    EU governments keep the GDPR's personal data definition

    EU member states agreed their Digital Omnibus negotiating mandate on 21 October, keeping the GDPR's existing definition of personal data, rejecting the Commission's proposed change to Article 4(1), adding a provision on pseudonymisation standards and attaching safeguards to processing personal data for AI training; nothing is law until Parliament sets its position and trilogue concludes. IAPP · 2026-10-22

    The hoped-for relief that pseudonymised data might fall outside the GDPR is off the table for now, so a roadmap that treats pseudonymisation as making data safe to hand a model should keep pseudonymised datasets inside the same access, lineage and purpose controls as raw personal data.

Managing Data Products at Enterprise Scale

A data product is a dataset someone has promised to look after for the people who use it, the way a shop promises the milk is fresh and in the same place tomorrow. That promise needs an owner, a description of what is inside, and a written agreement about what the consumer can rely on, which is what a data contract is. This three half-day online course walks through that lifecycle: who owns a data product, how it is changed and retired, and how contracts and metadata let other teams use and share it safely.

For the roadmap Most data product writing stops at the idea; this course spends its time on roles, processes and contracts, which is the part a platform roadmap has to fund. A PM deciding whether modelling authority sits centrally or with consuming domains can use its lifecycle as a checklist for what each side must own before the second governed dataset goes live.
